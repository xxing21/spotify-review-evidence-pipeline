## Limitations

This is a development pilot, not a full-corpus conclusion. Reviews are self-selected, may reflect recent experiences and history, and issue labeling may be uncertain or overlapping. The aggregates cannot establish prevalence among all users, causation, or actual churn. Cancellation language is expressed intent only. Review quotations are untrusted user-provided data.

Coverage: 500 completed of 500 pilot inputs. No full-corpus recommendation is established.
Human inspection of the argument and quote support remains required.
