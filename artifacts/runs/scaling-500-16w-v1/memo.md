# Spotify product decision — pilot only

Priority: **ISSUE-USABILITY**

Prioritize playback controls and navigation: it has the strongest aggregate priority signal, supported by many complaints despite lower average severity than some alternatives. Reviews describe difficulty changing tracks and seeking. App and streaming reliability is the strongest alternative on average severity, with reports of crashes and launch failures; premium restrictions also draw substantial complaints and may warrant a separate product review. Account access has high average severity but a smaller complaint signal in this pilot. These comparisons support a bounded investigation, not a claim about prevalence across Spotify users or the cause of any outcome.

Alternatives: ISSUE-PLAYBACK, ISSUE-BILLING, ISSUE-ACCESS

## Limitations

This is a development pilot, not a full-corpus finding. Reviewers self-select, and prior product history—especially reactions to updates—may shape what they report. The pilot is small, and issue labeling is uncertain; categories may overlap or capture different underlying problems. Cancellation language reflects stated intent only, not observed churn.

Coverage: 500 completed of 500 pilot inputs. No full-corpus recommendation is established.
Human inspection of the argument and quote support remains required.
