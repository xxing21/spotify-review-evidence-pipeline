## Limitations

This is a development pilot, not a full-corpus finding. Reviewers self-select, the evidence reflects a bounded historical window, and the pilot is limited in size. Issue labels can be uncertain or combine distinct concerns; the supplied examples are illustrative and may not represent all complaints. Cancellation statements express intent or reported action only, not observed churn or its cause.

Coverage: 10000 completed of 10000 pilot inputs. No full-corpus recommendation is established.
