Prioritize song selection and playback controls for investigation: this issue leads the pilot on aggregate priority, with more complaints and greater summed severity than playback failures, paid-plan restrictions, or broad dissatisfaction. Playback failures are a credible alternative because the sampled reports describe crashes and loading problems; paid-plan restrictions may overlap with usability rather than represent a separate problem. Mentions of switching, uninstalling, or canceling express intent only and do not establish actual churn or causation.
